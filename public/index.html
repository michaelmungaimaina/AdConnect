Privacy Policy

Effective: December 20th, 2024

At AdConnect, your privacy is a priority. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect Personal Information (name, email, phone), Usage Data (IP, browser, browsing behaviour via cookies, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel), and Device Information (device type, location if shared).

2. How We Use Your Data

To connect real estate companies with potential customers, analyse website performance, create targeted marketing campaigns, send service communications, and respond to inquiries.

3. Sharing Your Data

We may share data with marketing platforms (Facebook, Google) and service providers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to understand user behaviour, serve relevant ads, and measure campaign performance. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

We store personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes in this policy, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete your data, and opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us at info@adconnect.co.ke.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact AdConnect or email info@adconnect.co.ke.
