Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective: December 20th, 2024

At AdConnect, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you engage with our services, visit our website, or interact with our digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and details provided when filling out forms on our website.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and browsing behaviour through cookies and tracking technologies (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel).
  • Device Information: Device type, screen resolution, and geographic location if shared.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To help businesses grow through marketing, technology and data.
  • To analyse website performance and improve user experience.
  • To create targeted marketing campaigns through Facebook Pixel and similar tools.
  • To send updates, promotional offers, and service communications.
  • To respond to your enquiries or requests.

3. Sharing Your Data

We may share your data with marketing platforms (Facebook, Google) and service providers used for email marketing, analytics, or lead management. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to understand user behaviour, serve relevant ads, and measure campaign performance. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or via our cookie consent banner.

5. Data Retention

We store personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

6. Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. However, no method of online transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request access to your personal data.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

8. International Users

If you are accessing our services from outside Kenya, your information may be transferred, stored, and processed in Kenya. By using our services, you consent to this transfer.

9. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at: info@adconnect.co.ke or visit our Contact page.
